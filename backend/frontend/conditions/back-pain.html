Types of Back Pain We Help With

Back pain can affect anyone and may be caused by many different factors. We assess your individual needs and tailor treatment accordingly.

Lower Back Pain

Common and can affect daily activities.

Upper & Mid Back Pain

Related to posture, work or muscle strain.

Sciatica

Pain, tingling or numbness radiating to the legs.

Muscle Strain

Caused by lifting, sudden movements or overuse.

Postural Back Pain

Linked to prolonged sitting or poor ergonomics.

Work-Related Back Pain

Due to work postures or repetitive tasks.

How Physiotherapy Can Help

Our physiotherapists use a combination of hands-on treatment, movement-based exercises and education to:

  • Reduce pain and muscle tension
  • Improve flexibility, strength and posture
  • Restore normal movement and function
  • Address the root cause, not just the symptoms
  • Provide strategies to prevent recurrence

Common Symptoms

  • Pain or stiffness in the lower, mid or upper back
  • Muscle tightness or spasms
  • Difficulty with standing, walking or sitting
  • Pain that radiates to the legs (sciatica)
  • Reduced flexibility and movement

Common Causes

  • Poor posture (e.g., prolonged sitting)
  • Muscle strain or overuse
  • Sedentary lifestyle
  • Incorrect lifting techniques
  • Sports or work-related injuries
  • Age-related changes (e.g., disc degeneration)

When to Seek Urgent Medical Help

Please contact your GP or A&E immediately if you experience:

  • New problems with bladder or bowel control
  • Numbness around the groin or inner thighs (saddle area)
  • Sudden or rapidly worsening weakness in the legs
  • Severe pain after a significant injury (e.g. fall or accident)
  • Unexplained weight loss, fever or night pain
