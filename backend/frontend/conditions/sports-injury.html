UNDERSTAND YOUR INJURY

Sports Injury Overview

Sports injuries can affect strength, mobility, confidence and your ability to train or stay active. Your physiotherapist starts with a focused assessment of the injured area, movement, load tolerance and the activities you want to return to.

HOW WE HELP

How NEXmove Physio Can Help

We build a progressive rehabilitation plan around your injury, symptoms and goals. Treatment may include movement advice, strength and control exercises, gradual loading and sport-specific preparation so you can return to activity with greater confidence.

When to Seek Medical Advice

Seek prompt medical assessment if you experience:

  • Severe pain after a significant injury or fall
  • An obvious deformity or inability to use the injured limb
  • Rapidly increasing swelling, redness or heat
  • New numbness, marked weakness or loss of function
  • Symptoms that are worsening unexpectedly or making you feel unwell

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about sports injury physiotherapy.

When can I return to sport?+

Return to sport depends on the injury, your symptoms, strength, control and the demands of your activity. Your physiotherapist can guide a graded progression rather than relying on time alone.

How many sessions will I need?+

This varies with the injury, how long you have had symptoms, your goals and your response to rehabilitation. Progress is reviewed regularly so the plan can be adjusted.

Can physiotherapy help a recent sports injury?+

Yes, but the approach depends on the injury and whether medical assessment is needed first. Your physiotherapist can assess movement and function and guide appropriate early rehabilitation.

Can you treat me at home?+

Yes. Home visits can include assessment, rehabilitation exercises, movement retraining and advice using the space and equipment available to you.

Do you work with scan or specialist findings?+

Yes. If you have scan reports, discharge information or specialist guidance, your physiotherapist can consider these alongside your symptoms, examination and rehabilitation goals.
