Cookie Policy

This policy explains how NEXmove Physio uses cookies and similar technologies, including browser storage, on its website.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

What are cookies and website storage?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored on your device. Websites may also use browser storage, which has similar privacy implications. Some technologies are needed to provide the website or features you request. Other technologies, such as advertising trackers, may require your permission.

Necessary website storage

Our public website pages use browser local storage to remember that you have read the cookie notice. The item is called nexmove_cookie_notice_v1 and expires for notice purposes after 180 days. It does not contain your name, contact details or medical information.

Where you use an authenticated NEXmove account, we may use storage to keep you signed in and protect your session. Appointment, payment and account services may also use technical storage needed to provide the service you request. Such storage is separate from optional advertising tracking.

Analytics and advertising

No optional analytics or advertising tracking scripts were identified in the public information pages reviewed when this policy was prepared. We are not enabling any optional trackers through the cookie notice. If we introduce such technologies, we will update our disclosures and obtain consent or provide the legally required objection controls before their use.

Third-party services

Some pages request resources from other providers, for example web fonts. Booking and payment providers may also operate their own essential security or payment technologies when you use their services. Their practices may be described in their respective privacy notices. We review the technologies used on our website as services change.

Managing website storage

You can use your browser's site settings to view or delete cookies and local storage for this website. The Cookie settings control on NEXmove pages lets you reopen our information notice. Removing necessary storage may affect signed-in features or require you to sign in again.

The homepage notice is informational, not permission for future analytics or advertising cookies. If optional technologies are added, they must have their own appropriate controls.
