Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about home physiotherapy.

Book a Physio
How does a home visit work?+

Once you book, your physiotherapist arrives at your home at the scheduled time, completes an assessment, provides appropriate treatment and creates a personalised exercise plan.

Are all physiotherapists verified?+

Physiotherapists on the platform are checked for professional registration and relevant documentation before offering services.

Is physiotherapy covered by private health insurance?+

Some practitioners may accept private health insurance. Check your individual policy and insurer requirements before booking.

How quickly can I get an appointment?+

Availability depends on your location and practitioner. Same-day or next-day appointments may be available in some areas.

What should I prepare for a home visit?+

A clear space of roughly 2m × 2m is usually enough. Wear comfortable clothing and keep referral letters or scan results available if relevant.

Can I cancel or reschedule?+

Cancellation and rescheduling rules depend on the booking terms shown when you arrange your appointment.
