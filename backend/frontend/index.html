BEFORE YOU BOOK

Questions Patients Often Ask

Clear answers about what to expect from NEXmove home physiotherapy.

Home care, explained simply Know what happens before, during and after your appointment.
01 What can a home physiotherapist help with?

Home physiotherapy can support problems such as back or neck pain, joint and mobility difficulties, sports injuries, post-surgical recovery, neurological rehabilitation and age-related movement concerns. Your physiotherapist will assess whether home-based care is appropriate for you.

02 What happens at my first home appointment?

Your physiotherapist will discuss your symptoms and goals, assess your movement and function, explain their findings and agree a personalised treatment plan. Treatment and suitable exercises may also begin during the same visit.

03 Do I need a GP referral before I book?

In many cases you can book physiotherapy directly without a GP referral. If your symptoms or medical history suggest that another healthcare professional should review you first, your physiotherapist will explain the safest next step.

04 Can a family member or carer be present?

Yes. A relative, friend or carer can be present if you would find that helpful. This can be useful for discussing goals, mobility support, home exercises or day-to-day care needs.

05 What should I wear and prepare for the visit?

Wear comfortable clothing that allows you to move easily. Keep any relevant referral letters, scan reports, medication information or discharge documents nearby, and provide a safe clear space for assessment and exercises.

06 Will I need special equipment at home?

Usually not. Your physiotherapist can work with the space and everyday items available in your home and will tell you if any specific exercise or mobility equipment could be useful for your rehabilitation.

07 Can I continue with follow-up or online sessions?

Yes. Depending on your needs and progress, your care may include further home visits, follow-up appointments or suitable online consultations. Your physiotherapist can recommend the most practical option for the next stage of recovery.

08 What if my symptoms feel urgent or severe?

Home physiotherapy is not an emergency service. If symptoms are severe or life-threatening, call 999. For urgent medical advice that is not an emergency, call 111.
